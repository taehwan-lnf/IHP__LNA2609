# Datasheet

This die has not been fabricated yet. The MPW run has tape-in on 29 September 2026 and the
estimated shipment date is 31 May 2027, so no measured data exists at the time of writing.

This file will be filled with the measured results once the samples have been received.
The planned content is:

- DC Gummel and output characteristics of each device under test
- De-embedded S-parameters over the measured frequency range, with the de-embedding
  procedure stated
- Extracted small-signal parameters and the transit frequency against collector current
- A comparison of the measurements against the compact models shipped with the PDK

The raw measurement files, the setup description and the scripts that process them will be
published under `measurements/v.1.0.0/` alongside this file.
